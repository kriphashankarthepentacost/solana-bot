Money plan

The bot sizes everything to the money it really has and the most you accept to lose. Change the limit in Telegram: /money 200.

Risk radar

Every hour the bot reads the whole market and its own form, and decides how much risk to take. It changes the size of new trades by itself and goes back to normal by itself; open trades always keep their stop-losses.

The bot's own review of its trades

Before each trade it writes why and how it will get out; after the trade it checks what happened.

What the bot learned

Once a week the bot reviews its own results. It changes nothing by itself: each idea comes as a proposal with proof, and you decide in Telegram (/approve 3, /reject 3, /undo 3).

The brain

Decisions above the single strategies: the market's mood, which strategies are resting, and the bot's chance of a big loss in the next 30 days (from thousands of simulated futures).

Coin checks: was each decision right?

Every new coin the sniper and pump.fun looked at, priced again 24 hours later. Skipped coins that mostly fell = the filter saved money; skipped coins that doubled = it cost a win.
